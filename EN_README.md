📖 What is it?

Drama Mill is a dedicated all-in-one AI generator and workspace built for content creators. It seamlessly integrates traditional screenwriting, character design, storyboarding, video generation, and post-editing into one fluid workflow. Starting from a single sentence of inspiration or an imported draft, you can use AI to break it down frame-by-frame and generate a continuous video masterpiece.

🎯 What problems does it solve?

When creating AI videos, especially "AI Shorts," creators usually face these pain points:

Severe Tool Fragmentation: Using ChatGPT for scripts, Midjourney for images, Runway/Kling for video, and yet another tool for voiceovers makes project management a nightmare.

Inconsistent Characters/Scenes (Morphing/Hallucinations): It is notoriously difficult for AI to maintain the exact appearance of a character or background across consecutive, distinct shots.

Uncontrollable Camerawork: AI is highly randomized, making it difficult to precisely control shot framing (e.g., close-up vs. wide shot) and camera movement.

High Cost of Modification: A minor tweak to a single line of dialogue or visual element often requires re-rendering the entire video.

💡 The Solution: Drama Mill perfectly solves these issues by building a standard pipeline: "Script -> Character Design -> Structured Storyboard -> Shot-by-Shot Synthesis." It downgrades AI back to a tool, putting you firmly back in the "Director's Chair."

🚀 Key Features

1. 📝 AI Script Generator

Inspiration Mode: Enter a one-sentence idea, and the AI automatically expands it into a standard script complete with scenes, speakers, and action cues.

Original Draft Mode: Import an existing novel or script. The system parses and extracts characters, scenes, and dialogue without forcing an AI rewrite of your original work.

2. 🎭 Character & Scene Consistency

Character Design: Generate and confirm "Character Concept Art" first.

Scene Fixing: Establish reference images for scenes (e.g., "rainy street").

Mechanism: For all subsequent shots, the AI strictly reuses these references to ensure faces don't morph and backgrounds remain perfectly consistent.

3. 🎬 AI Storyboard Generator

Shot-by-Shot Breakdown: Splices the plot into individual, editable storyboards (shots).

Professional Controllable Parameters: Uses constrained vocabularies instead of wild text prompts. Supports 8 shot sizes, 36 camera movements, and exact durations. Use a wide shot to establish the setting and a close-up to convey emotion.

Continuous Sequence Planning: Ensures visuals remain seamless at transition points.

4. 🎞️ Video Synthesis & Modification (Shot-to-Video)

Non-Destructive Editing: If you are unhappy with a single shot, you do not need to remake the entire episode. You can adjust parameters (dialogue, framing) just for that specific shot, or even swap it with an alternative take from your generation history.

Audio-Visual Sync: Combines voiceovers and subtitles for a final review of the synthesized video.
